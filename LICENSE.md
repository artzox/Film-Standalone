### HexLensFlare Algorithm

The hexagonal lens flare implementation is a port of the HexLensFlare ReShade
shader. Used under the terms of its original license.

by Ganossa (mediehawk@gmail.com), specifically the `PS_AL_DetectHigh` colour
treatment, 5-tap separable Gaussian blur, and scene luminance adaptation.
